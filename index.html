Toronto Water Fountain Finder — Privacy disclosure

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This independent app helps you find City-operated water sources in Toronto parks. It does not require an account, use a developer-operated server, or include an analytics SDK.

Location

Location permission is optional. The app shows its cached fountain list first, then asks once on first launch whether it may use location. If granted, a foreground, one-time location request supports distances and map centring on launch and when returning to the app. Approximate location is sufficient. Coordinates are held in memory, cleared when the activity stops, and are not sent to a developer-operated service. You can revoke permission in Android settings and keep browsing.

Local storage and network services

The app stores public fountain data and download timestamps in its private local database for offline access. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove this cache. Android app backup is disabled.

Google Maps SDK and Google Play services may process device, diagnostic, network and location information under Google's privacy policy. The City data service receives normal network request information when the app downloads public data. Choosing Directions sends destination coordinates to Google Maps or your browser; that external app manages routing and its own permissions.

The developer does not receive your search terms, location history or saved fountain usage. Search is performed locally. No background location permission is requested.

Data limitations

City reports and seasonal estimates can be outdated. Downloading data does not inspect a fountain. Offline maps and external directions are not guaranteed.
