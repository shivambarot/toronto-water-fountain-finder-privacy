Toronto Water & Washrooms — Privacy disclosure

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This independent app helps you find water sources and public-use washrooms operated by Toronto Parks and Recreation. Coverage is limited to the official datasets, not every public washroom in Toronto. It does not require an account, use a developer-operated server, or include an analytics SDK.

Location

Location permission is optional. The app shows its cached location data first, then asks once on first launch whether it may use location. If granted, a foreground, one-time location request supports distances and map centring on launch and when returning to the app. Approximate location is sufficient. Coordinates are held in memory, cleared when the activity stops, and are not sent to a developer-operated service. You can revoke permission in Android settings and keep browsing.

Local storage and network services

The app stores public water and washroom data and download timestamps in its private local database for offline access. Water is checked quietly on launch after 14 days; washrooms after 24 hours. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove this cache. Android app backup is disabled.

Google Maps SDK and Google Play services may process device, diagnostic, network and location information under Google's privacy policy. The City data service receives normal network request information when the app downloads public data. Choosing Directions sends destination coordinates to Google Maps or your browser; that external app manages routing and its own permissions.

The developer does not receive your search terms, location history or saved location usage. Search is performed locally. No background location permission is requested.

Data limitations

Official sources: Parks Drinking Fountains and Washroom Facilities. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the City of Toronto or any government agency.

City reports and seasonal estimates can be outdated. Downloading data does not inspect a facility. Offline maps and external directions are not guaranteed.
